# Article Structure

Use this structure when creating or normalizing files in `docs/referrence/`.

## Recommended sections
1. Intro
   - Explain what the technology, framework, company, or research theme is.
2. Current frontier
   - Summarize current capabilities, practical deployment level, ecosystem state, or benchmark significance.
3. Future direction
   - Summarize what the field or vendor is trying to achieve next.
4. Practical implications
   - Explain how the topic may affect architecture, MVP choices, product strategy, operational constraints, or research direction.
5. Sources
   - List primary or clearly attributable sources when available.

## Subject-specific emphasis
- Protocol / standard:
  - Focus on interoperability, ecosystem adoption, performance, governance, and safety.
- Framework / methodology:
  - Focus on where it fits in a real delivery process, what it improves, and what tooling supports it.
- Company / platform:
  - Focus on actual shipped capability, power/performance or cost constraints, and realistic use cases.
- Research direction:
  - Focus on what is demonstrated now versus what remains open.

## Tone
- Japanese by default.
- Technical, but readable for future implementation planning.
- Avoid overstating immature claims.
